# libSceLibreSsl3

## add Functional

- [`EVP_PKEY_CTX_ctrl` on EC contexts](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceLibreSsl3/src/PkeyContext.cpp) (libSceLibreSsl3) - console behaviour is unverified. `EVP_PKEY_CTRL_EC_KDF_TYPE` with `EVP_PKEY_ECDH_KDF_X9_63` throws: the X9.63 KDF is not modelled. `EVP_PKEY_CTRL_EC_ECDH_COFACTOR` with 0 or 1 throws on a group whose cofactor is not 1, where LibreSSL copies the key into a cofactor key; no group `libSceLibreSsl` builds has one. `EVP_PKEY_CTRL_EC_PARAMGEN_CURVE_NID` throws for the LibreSSL 3.4.1 curves that `libSceLibreSsl` cannot build: the 79 mbedtls lacks and the GOST R 34.10-2012 curves (NIDs 993 to 998). A null digest, a null getter output or a missing key for `EVP_PKEY_CTRL_EC_ECDH_COFACTOR` throws where LibreSSL dereferences it. Otherwise follows `pkey_ec_ctrl`, `pkey_ec_init`, `pkey_ec_cleanup` and the curve list of the submodule's LibreSSL 3.4.1. Closes when: the X9.63 KDF and the cofactor key are modelled and compared with the console.

## change [`EVP_PKEY_derive_init`, `EVP_PKEY_derive_set_peer` and `EVP_PKEY_derive`]

- [`EVP_PKEY_derive_init`, `EVP_PKEY_derive_set_peer` and `EVP_PKEY_derive`](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceLibreSsl3/src/PkeyContext.cpp) (libSceLibreSsl3) - console ABI and error-queue parity are unverified. They follow LibreSSL 4.0.0 `pmeth_fn.c` and `ec_pmeth.c` at [`ce488b78e0bd994780569df718596b9776ff681a`](https://github.com/libressl/openbsd/tree/ce488b78e0bd994780569df718596b9776ff681a/src/lib/libcrypto) for `EVP_PKEY_EC`. Because `libSceLibreSsl` `ECDH_compute_key` rejects short buffers and zeroes long-buffer tails whereas LibreSSL 4.0.0 truncates to `*keylen`, `EcDerive` computes into a 66-byte stack buffer and copies `min(*keylen, secretlen)` without writing past `*keylen`. Selecting the X9.63 KDF, or ECDH cofactor mode 0 or 1 on a group whose cofactor is not 1, through `EVP_PKEY_CTX_ctrl` throws, so the derivation never applies a KDF or a cofactor key; on a group with cofactor 1 the cofactor mode changes nothing, as in LibreSSL. Closes when: compared with the console.
